Judges FAQ

Canteen kyun judega?

Zyada orders, chhoti line, digital hisaab aur unka koi investment nahi.

Paisa kaise kamayenge?

5% commission, ₹10 delivery fee aur local ads se.

Zomato/Swiggy se alag kaise?

Sirf campus ke liye, canteen rate, sasti delivery aur tez service.

Payment safe hai?

Hum trusted UPI gateway use karenge; card details store nahi karte.

Internet na ho to?

Token system aur counter par cash ka backup.
